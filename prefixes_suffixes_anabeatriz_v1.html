Como funciona

PREFIXOS (mudam o sentido):
un- / dis- / in- / im- / ir- / il- = não, o contrário  ·  mis- = errado  ·  re- = de novo  ·  over- = demais  ·  under- = de menos  ·  pre- = antes

SUFIXOS (mudam a classe da palavra):
substantivo: -tion / -sion · -ment · -ness · -ity  ·  adjetivo: -able · -ful · -less · -al  ·  verbo: -en · -ize

💡 Você já usou uma dessas: underestimate = subestimar (estimar de menos)

Parte 1 — Prefixos

Complete com a palavra entre parênteses + o prefixo certo. Atenção ao tempo verbal!

  1. A lot of people (estimate) the power of taking a break.
  2. I think I (do) it at the gym yesterday — I can barely walk today.
  3. My client (understand) the instructions and did the whole exercise on the wrong side.
  4. That viral theory about drinking olive oil every morning sounds totally (believable).
  5. After two weeks off, I came back feeling completely (charged).
  6. Skipping the warm-up before a heavy class is just (responsible).
  7. Bad posture can create a muscle (balance) in your back.
  8. I totally (agree) with people who say resting is a waste of time.
  9. Always do a quick (workout) stretch — your joints will thank you.
1. underestimate  |  2. overdid  |  3. misunderstood  |  4. unbelievable  |  5. recharged  |  6. irresponsible  |  7. imbalance  |  8. disagree  |  9. pre-workout

Dica: im- vem antes de b, m, p (imbalance, impatient, impossible) e ir- antes de r (irresponsible, irregular).
"I overdid it" = exagerei, passei do limite. Super comum depois de treino, comida, festa…

Parte 2 — Sufixos

Transforme a palavra entre parênteses pra ela caber na frase (substantivo, adjetivo ou verbo).

  1. Pilates is really (benefit) for people with back pain.
  2. Her (flexible) improved so much in three months!
  3. Taking a year off was the best (decide) I've ever made.
  4. Moving to another country on your own is a huge (accomplish).
  5. We need to (strong) your core before you try that exercise.
  6. Don't trust every news site — not all of them are (rely).
  7. Arguing with conspiracy theorists online is completely (point).
  8. He's so (care) — he never pays attention to his posture.
  9. There's a lot more (aware) about burnout now than ten years ago.
1. beneficial  |  2. flexibility  |  3. decision  |  4. accomplishment  |  5. strengthen  |  6. reliable  |  7. pointless  |  8. careless  |  9. awareness

Repare: strong → strength → strengthen (o verbo vem do substantivo). Mesma lógica: long → length → lengthen, wide → width → widen.

Parte 3 — Qual palavra existe?

Escolha a única opção que existe em inglês.

1. My new client is so ______ — she wants a six-pack in one week.
2. Sorry, I'm ______ to make it to class today. I'm sick.
3. That theory is totally ______. There's zero evidence for it.
4. Don't ______ the reformer springs — they can snap.
5. Lots of physical therapists in Brazil feel ______ for how hard they work.
6. Thank you so much for the tips — you've been super ______!
1. impatient  |  2. unable  |  3. illogical  |  4. misuse  |  5. underpaid  |  6. helpful

Nº 2: disabled existe, mas significa "com deficiência", não "incapaz de ir".
Nº 3: il- vem antes de l (illogical, illegal).

Parte 5 — Should I feel bad about it?

Escolha uma das perguntas e escreva de 6 a 8 frases dando sua opinião. Use pelo menos 6 palavras com prefixo ou sufixo desta lista (sublinhe ou coloque em MAIÚSCULA pra professora ver). Depois, a gente conversa sobre isso na aula.

a) Is it irresponsible to take a year off from your career?

b) Why do people believe unbelievable things they see on TikTok?

c) Do people underestimate how important rest is for the body?

Banco de palavras: overdo · underestimate · recharge · unbelievable · irresponsible · disagree · misunderstand · beneficial · decision · accomplishment · reliable · pointless · careless · awareness · uncomfortable · powerful · unmotivated

Resposta pessoal. Exemplo (c):
"Honestly, I think most people UNDERESTIMATE how much the body needs to rest. As a physical therapist, I see clients who OVERDO it all the time — they train every day and then they're surprised when they get hurt. It's not POINTLESS to take a day off; it's actually really BENEFICIAL. Taking a break was the best DECISION I've ever made. I came back feeling RECHARGED and much more MOTIVATED. There's more AWARENESS about burnout now, but we still feel guilty for resting."

Pontos pra corrigir: -ful só tem um L (careful, não "carefull") · ordem certa: prefixo antes, sufixo depois (un-believ-able).
